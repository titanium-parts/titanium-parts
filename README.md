## Engineering Philosophy

I believe good engineering is intentional.

Technology should solve real problems, not introduce unnecessary complexity. Architecture should be understandable, systems should be maintainable, and engineering decisions should be explainable.

Some principles I work by:

- **Keep it simple.** Complexity should be justified, not celebrated.
- **Understand the problem first.** Technology selection comes after understanding the requirements.
- **Make ownership explicit.** Systems need clear accountability and decision-making authority.
- **Engineer for maintainability.** Someone else will eventually need to understand what you've built.
- **Build governance into the process.** Documentation, testing, security, and review are engineering responsibilities.
- **Favor repeatability.** Sustainable processes are better than relying on individual heroics.
- **Embrace iteration.** Engineering is a process of learning, testing, correcting, and improving.

I believe consistency matters more than perfection, and that experience is knowing which shortcuts are reasonable and which ones will eventually become expensive.
